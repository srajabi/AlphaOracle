These tariffs are explicitly linked to “raising chances of the Fed keeping interest rates higher for longer.” “World stocks tumble as Trump tariffs rattle markets, AI rally fades.”

      • Risk-Off (SPY, GLD, ^VIX): Trade policy shocks are a clear driver of risk aversion.
