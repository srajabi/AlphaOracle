The market, despite being in a “Bull Quiet” regime, is showing nuanced price action. Major indices (SPY, QQQ, VOO) are in long-term uptrends but exhibit short-term bearish momentum/consolidation, pulling back from recent highs towards their short-term moving averages. This suggests a potential for continued short-term mean reversion downwards or a consolidation phase within the broader uptrend.

Energy (XLE) and Gold/Silver (GLD, IAU, SLV) show strong trend continuation, with XLE showing robust strength and GLD/IAU/SLV showing bullish momentum but facing SMA_200 resistance or minor deceleration. This confirms the “Commodities strong defensive” aspect of the intermarket indicators.

Many “AI plays” (NVDA, AMD, INTC, AVGO, TSM, NBIS, STX) are in longer-term uptrends but are experiencing short-term pullbacks, some with significant bearish momentum (NBIS, AVGO, INTC, STX, KLAC). PLTR, CRWD, AAPL, MSFT, ORCL, MU, IBIT are showing stronger bullish momentum, with some (IBIT, CRWD, AAPL, MSFT, PLTR) appearing stretched near upper Bollinger Bands or overbought RSI, suggesting they might be due for a pause or pullback.

Utilities (XLU, TLN) are showing clear downtrends or breakdowns, indicating capital rotation out of defensive sectors. Bonds (TLT, TMF, LQD, HYG) are mixed, with TLT and TMF showing short-term bounces within longer-term downtrends (as yields rise, ^TNX uptrend). HYG is in tight consolidation.

The low and falling VIX confirms the “quiet” aspect of the regime, but internal sector rotation and short-term mean reversion in broad indices suggest a more complex picture than just straight upward movement. Some tickers are exhibiting signs of volatility squeezes (HYG, VGK, XLB, EWA), implying potential for larger moves once a direction is established.
