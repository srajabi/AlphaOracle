However, for most, current technicals suggest further downside is plausible.

    • Long Calls (GLD, QQQ, SPY): Long calls on GLD, QQQ, SPY are directional bets on upside.     • Long Puts (GLD, QQQ, SPY): Long puts on GLD, QQQ, SPY align with the prevailing bearish trends. The spreads on SPY and QQQ options are generally tight, indicating good liquidity for these index products.

Overall Options Recommendation: In a “Bear Volatile” regime with confirmed equity downtrends, selling OTM cash-secured puts is risky, as desired entry levels could be breached. Long puts on equity indices like SPY and QQQ are more aligned with the current technical momentum for downside protection or bearish plays.
